01. Quick Start Guide

Universal PC Remote™ is designed to be up and running in under three minutes without creating accounts or setting up cloud tunnels.

1

Extract the Windows Companion

Download UniversalPCRemote-v1.0.0-windows-x64.zip (77.6 MB). Extract the folder anywhere on your PC (such as Desktop or Documents).

💡
Zero Installation Required: The companion is completely portable. Simply double-click UniversalPCRemote.exe to launch. It runs silently in your Windows System Tray (notification area).
2

Install the Android Client

Download UniversalPCRemote.apk (23.6 MB) directly to your Android device, or scan the official QR code on the homepage. Open the downloaded file to install it.

If prompted by Android, tap Settings and enable "Allow from this source" for your browser or file manager to complete the installation.

3

Connect & Pair

Make sure your phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi network. Launch the Android app and tap "Discover PCs". Your computer will be detected automatically via UDP broadcast beacon. Tap your PC and enter the 6-digit PIN displayed on your computer screen to complete pairing.

02. Pairing Walkthrough & Discovery

Universal PC Remote™ utilizes a multi-stage discovery and cryptographic pairing architecture to ensure fast connection without compromising security.

How Automatic Discovery Works

When you tap "Discover PCs" on your Android phone, the app broadcasts a lightweight discovery datagram to the local subnet on UDP port 51235. When the Windows companion receives this beacon, it immediately answers with:

  • PC Hostname: Your Windows computer's name (e.g. MY-GAMING-RIG).
  • Local LAN IP: The active network adapter's IP address (e.g. 192.168.1.105).
  • WebSocket Port: The active connection port (default 51234).

Connecting via Manual IP Address (Fallback)

If your Wi-Fi router disables UDP broadcast or has AP isolation enabled, you can connect directly via IP:

  1. Open the Windows Companion dashboard by clicking the system tray icon, or open Command Prompt and type ipconfig.
  2. Find your IPv4 Address (usually starts with 192.168.x.x or 10.0.x.x).
  3. In the Android app, select "Manual Connect" and enter this IP address along with port 51234.

The 6-Digit Cryptographic Pairing Handshake

To prevent unauthorized devices on your Wi-Fi from controlling your mouse and keyboard:

  • When an unknown phone attempts to connect, the host PC displays a prominent notification dialog containing a random 6-digit one-time PIN.
  • Enter this PIN on your phone within 60 seconds.
  • The companion and phone exchange a cryptographically secure SHA-256 session token.
  • The token is stored in the app's secure keystore, allowing seamless instant reconnection in future sessions without re-entering the PIN.
  • You can revoke authorized devices at any time directly from the companion dashboard.

03. Windows Firewall Configuration

Because Universal PC Remote™ operates as a local network server, Windows Defender Firewall must permit incoming traffic on ports 51234 (WebSocket TCP) and 51235 (Discovery UDP).

ℹ️
First-Time Launch Prompt: The first time you open UniversalPCRemote.exe, Windows Firewall may display a pop-up asking "Allow Universal PC Remote to communicate on these networks?". Check "Private networks (home or work)" and click "Allow access".

Method A: One-Click Automated Script (Recommended)

The Windows Companion zip bundle includes an automated firewall configuration script (setup_firewall.bat):

  1. Locate setup_firewall.bat inside your extracted companion folder.
  2. Right-click setup_firewall.bat and select "Run as administrator".
  3. The script automatically registers inbound firewall rules for TCP port 51234 and UDP port 51235 in Windows Defender.
BAT • setup_firewall.bat 
netsh advfirewall firewall add rule name="Universal PC Remote (TCP-51234)" dir=in action=allow protocol=TCP localport=51234 profile=private
netsh advfirewall firewall add rule name="Universal PC Remote (UDP-51235)" dir=in action=allow protocol=UDP localport=51235 profile=private

Method B: Manual Windows Defender Firewall Configuration

If your corporate policy or custom security suite requires manual rule definition:

  1. Press Win + R, type wf.msc, and hit Enter to open Windows Defender Firewall with Advanced Security.
  2. Click Inbound Rules in the left panel, then click New Rule... on the right.
  3. Select Port > Click Next.
  4. Choose TCP and specify port 51234.
  5. Select Allow the connection > Click Next.
  6. Ensure Private is checked > Name the rule Universal PC Remote TCP.
  7. Repeat the exact same steps for UDP port 51235.

Verifying Windows Wi-Fi Profile is "Private"

Windows blocks local LAN communication if your Wi-Fi network profile is set to "Public". To verify:

  1. Open Windows Settings (Win + I) > Network & internet > Wi-Fi.
  2. Click on your connected Wi-Fi network name.
  3. Under Network profile type, make sure Private network is selected.

04. Troubleshooting Matrix

Encountering an issue? Find your symptom below to apply the exact verified resolution.

🔍 PC Not Found During "Discover PCs" Scan
Likely Cause: Router AP/Client Isolation is enabled, or phone is on a 5 GHz guest network while the PC is on a 2.4 GHz main network.
Resolution:
  • Log into your Wi-Fi router admin page and disable "AP Isolation" or "Client Isolation". This feature prevents wireless devices from seeing each other.
  • Ensure both devices are on the exact same Wi-Fi SSID (not a Guest network).
  • Use the Manual Connect option in the app by typing your PC's IP address directly (e.g. 192.168.1.50:51234).
🚫 Connection Timed Out or "Connection Refused"
Likely Cause: Companion is not running, or another program is occupying port 51234, or firewall blocked the connection.
Resolution:
  • Check your Windows System Tray (near the clock) to confirm the Universal PC Remote icon is visible.
  • Verify port 51234 status in PowerShell by running: netstat -ano | findstr 51234. If another app is listening, close it.
  • Run setup_firewall.bat as Administrator to refresh firewall permits.
🌐 Virtual Network Adapters Selecting Wrong IP
Likely Cause: Hyper-V, VirtualBox, VMware, or WSL created virtual network adapters that confuse automatic IP detection.
Resolution:
  • Click the Universal PC Remote system tray icon to view active network adapters.
  • Select your physical Wi-Fi or Ethernet adapter from the dropdown list.
  • Or look up your physical Wi-Fi IPv4 address with ipconfig and use Manual Connect on Android.
🛡️ Active VPN Tunnel Intercepting Local Routing
Likely Cause: Commercial or corporate VPNs (e.g. NordVPN, ExpressVPN, Cisco AnyConnect, Tailscale) routed all LAN packets through their tunnel.
Resolution:
  • In your VPN client settings, enable "Allow LAN traffic" or "Inbound LAN Connections" (Split Tunneling).
  • Temporarily disconnect the VPN during remote sessions to verify if routing was intercepted.
⚡ Touchpad Lag, Jitter, or Connection Drops After Phone Display Off
Likely Cause: Android OS aggressive battery management suspended the background Wi-Fi radio or killed the socket.
Resolution:
  • On Android, go to Settings > Apps > Universal PC Remote.
  • Tap Battery > Change from "Optimized" to "Unrestricted".
  • Connect both PC and phone to 5 GHz or 6 GHz Wi-Fi instead of congested 2.4 GHz channels for sub-8ms response.

Understanding Windows SmartScreen ("Windows Protected Your PC")

When running newly released, free open-source software, Microsoft Defender SmartScreen may display a blue dialog stating: "Windows protected your PC. Microsoft Defender SmartScreen prevented an unrecognized app from starting."

Why does this happen? Microsoft requires software publishers to purchase expensive corporate Extended Validation (EV) code-signing certificates ($400-$700/year) to establish instant reputation. Independent, free open-source projects without corporate certificates trigger this standard warning until hundreds of users run the application.

How to safely proceed:

  1. Click "More info" on the SmartScreen dialog.
  2. Verify that the application name displays UniversalPCRemote.exe.
  3. Click the "Run anyway" button.
  4. You can also upload UniversalPCRemote.exe directly to VirusTotal.com to scan it against 70+ antivirus engines.

06. Connection Security & LAN Hardening

Operating without a cloud server means your security perimeter is your local Wi-Fi network. Here is how we ensure secure operation:

  • Zero External Ingress: The companion binds strictly to local network interfaces. It does not configure UPnP port forwarding on your router and cannot be accessed from outside your home network.
  • Physical Proximity Defense: Device pairing requires entering a 6-digit PIN physically shown on the Windows desktop monitor. An attacker cannot pair without visual sight of your monitor.
  • Recommended Network Setup: For maximum security on untrusted public Wi-Fi (such as coffee shops or university campus networks), enable Windows Mobile Hotspot and connect your phone directly to your PC's dedicated hotspot network.
