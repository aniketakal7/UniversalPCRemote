Getting Started

Set up in
three simple steps.

Zero account creation. Zero firewall port forwarding. Up and running in less than 60 seconds.

STEP 01 ↓

Download both apps

Install the official Android remote APK on your phone and unpack the lightweight Windows companion on your PC.

STEP 02 ⌁

Connect to local Wi-Fi

Ensure both your phone and PC are connected to the same Wi-Fi router or phone mobile hotspot. UDP auto-discovery finds your PC automatically.

STEP 03 🔒

Enter 6-digit PIN

Type the 6-digit cryptographic PIN displayed on your Windows screen into your phone to establish an encrypted, private session.
