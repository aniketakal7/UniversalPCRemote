Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Does this require an internet connection? +
No! Universal PC Remote operates 100% locally over your Wi-Fi router or your phone's mobile hotspot. It does not send any packets over the internet.
Can I use my phone's mobile hotspot if there is no Wi-Fi router? +
Yes! Simply turn on your phone's portable hotspot, connect your Windows PC to the hotspot Wi-Fi, and the companion app will automatically discover and pair seamlessly.
What if Windows Firewall blocks the connection? +
The Windows Companion includes a 1-click button labeled "Allow in Windows Firewall" on the Dashboard. Clicking it automatically registers the private network rules for ports 51234 (WebSocket) and 51235 (Discovery).
How does the 6-digit PIN pairing work? +
When your phone requests to link with your PC, the PC displays a temporary 6-digit PIN. Entering this PIN on your phone completes a cryptographic SHA-256 token exchange and whitelists your device so neighbors on the same Wi-Fi cannot control your screen.
